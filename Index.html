Structured 4-Level Training Roadmap

LEVEL 1

Concept Building

Understanding core rules, piece movements, fundamental strategies, and tactical board concepts.

LEVEL 2

Simple Position Practice

Applying concepts in simple board positions to build calculation habits and basic checkmate patterns.

LEVEL 3

Complex Positions

Tackling multi-step tactical combinations, middlegame planning, and defending tricky positions.

LEVEL 4

Pattern Recall & Speed

Repeated solving to build rapid pattern recall speed, execution accuracy, and competitive tournament readiness.
